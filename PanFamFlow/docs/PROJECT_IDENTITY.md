# Project identity

**Name:** PanFamFlow

**Primary objective:** reproducible analysis of one configured target gene family across multiple assembled and annotated genomes or materials.

**Primary unit:** a target-family gene, its subfamily, its selected HOG membership and its family-level occupancy class.

**Not the objective:** genome assembly, graph-pangenome construction, genome-wide SV/PAV calling or a general whole-genome pan-gene catalog.
